Direction 01 / kinetic object

The Counterweight

A tiny editorial sculpture turns the existing brand mark into a physical object. The beam arrives, settles imperfectly, and leaves the two wallet choices completely still and equal.

The memorable moment
A miniature mobile seems suspended inside a printed instrument case.
Art to produce
Custom vector sculpture, subtle paper grain, restrained beam entrance.
Product fit
Closest to the current warm cream identity. Decorative geometry never claims a live balance.

Direction 02 / luminous threshold

The Aperture

The invitation becomes a quiet, almost ceremonial opening. An etched iris of plum and violet rotates slowly around a warm center; the actual wallet choice sits on a stable instrument panel below it.

The memorable moment
A living aperture opens as the modal appears, then becomes ambient.
Art to produce
Native vector rings, precision linework, light bloom. No video or 3D dependency.
Product fit
Most immersive and premium; should stay short and still during wallet authentication.

Direction 03 / expressive print

The Entry Folio

A boldly typeset, collectible-feeling access folio. Oversized words, a hand-cut seal, and a printed stripe make the popup feel like a designed artifact while the two routes remain plainly named.

The memorable moment
The “STARTED” line slides into registration like a printing press landing on paper.
Art to produce
Typographic composition, bespoke seal, fine paper grain and stripe.
Product fit
Fastest, clearest path to production; strongest link to the Entry Ticket system.
